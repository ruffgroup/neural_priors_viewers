# neural_priors_viewers

Standalone interactive [pycortex](https://github.com/gallantlab/pycortex)
WebGL viewers of numerosity receptive field parameters for all 39
participants of:

> Prat-Carrabin A.\*, de Hollander G.\*, Bedi S., Gershman S.J., &
> Ruff C.C. (2025). *Distributed range adaptation in human parietal
> encoding of numbers.* bioRxiv.
> [10.1101/2025.09.25.675916](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.25.675916)

Browse the viewers at **https://ruffgroup.github.io/neural_priors_viewers/**

Each viewer (`model3/sub-XX/`) shows, on the participant's native cortical
surface: cross-validated R² of the encoding model and preferred numerosity
(μ) in the narrow and wide range conditions (model 3: per-voxel fitted
shift ratio).

The viewers are generated by
[`neural_priors/visualize/make_static_viewers.py`](https://github.com/ruffgroup/neural_priors/blob/main/neural_priors/visualize/make_static_viewers.py)
in the analysis repository.
